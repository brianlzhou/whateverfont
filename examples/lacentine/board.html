Lacentine: 2 directions

Make me a font for Valentine's Day, like a card glued on a paper doily. Lace edges and small punched hearts, and letters that still read. - **What is it for?** A heading or a few words at a large size: a card, a bakery window, a school valentine, a sign for a February sale. Not for running text. (I chose this.) - **What should it feel like?** Warm, sweet, homemade. It should say Valentine's Day at a glance. (I chose this.) - **What must it not look like?** Kitsch that hides the letters, a font of heart pictures, or a script. The letters must read first and the ornament second. (I chose this.) - **Which styles?** One style in each of two families. `Lacentine` is a color font with four palettes. `Lacentine Card` is the same letters in one color. A color font has no variable file. (I chose this.)
