Pinout

Monospaced fonts with more character than a plain coding face, in the spirit of the return to typewriter and early-computer looks that recent coding fonts made popular. This one reads the brief as the technical documents of early computers. - **What it is for:** code, tables and terminals that should feel like a 1975 to 1985 datasheet, a terminal manual or a machine listing. (I chose this reading. The person did not say.) - **How it should feel:** engineered, square, tight but clear. Read at a glance in a table of figures. - **What it must not look like:** Tally Mono, the plain monospace of this repository. No rounded corners. - **Styles:** Light, Regular and Bold on a weight axis.
