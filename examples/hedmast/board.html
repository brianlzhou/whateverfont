Hedmast

Make me a tall, narrow sans for newspaper headlines. Heavy enough to shout, and still clear in a long headline. - **What it is for:** headlines of five to ten words at 40 to 140 pixels, in capitals or in mixed case, on news pages, posters, video titles and sports scores. The Regular also sets a short paragraph at 18 pixels. - **How it should feel:** loud and plain. A long headline must stay easy to read in the heaviest weight. - **What it must not look like:** Caliper, the light condensed flare serif in `examples/caliper`. Hedmast has no flare and its weights go much heavier. - **Styles:** Regular, Medium, Semibold, Bold and Heavy, and one variable font with a weight axis and a width axis. No italic: the brief did not ask for one.
