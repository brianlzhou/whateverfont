Panework: 2 directions

Make me a font like Mondrian's grid paintings. Black lines divide each letter into rectangles: most of them white, a few of them red, yellow or blue. The source is the grid paintings of Piet Mondrian and the De Stijl movement, such as "Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow", and the late "Broadway Boogie Woogie". Each letter is such a painting: thick horizontal and vertical lines divide it into rectangles, most of them white, a few of them red, yellow or blue. - **What is it for?** A few words at a large size: a gallery poster, a museum sign, a jazz flyer, a title. Not running text. (I chose this.) - **What should it feel like?** Bright, modern, balanced, a little playful. (I chose this.) - **What must it not look like?** A plain sans with a checkerboard on it, a pixel font, or a letter that the grid makes hard to read. No curved or diagonal lines inside a letter. (I chose this.) - **Which styles?** One style in each of two families: `Panework` (the classic grids) and `Panework Boogie` (the late, busy grid). A color font has no variable file. (I chose this.) I worked from what is known about the paintings: only horizontal and vertical lines, unequal rectangles, a balance without symmetry, large quiet white areas, one or two color fields per picture, lines that sometimes stop short of the edge, and in the late painting, yellow lines broken by small squares of red, blue and gray. I did not open, trace or sample any image.
