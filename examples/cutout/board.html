Cutout: 2 directions

Make me a font like Matisse's paper cut-outs. Big bold shapes that look cut with scissors out of painted paper, a bit wobbly, in flat bright colors (that blue, plus coral, yellow, green, black), each letter its own color, maybe with a few leaf or seaweed shapes. Joyful. It's for a children's museum sign and a festival poster. - **What is it for?** A few large words: the sign of a children's museum and the title, date and time on a festival poster. Not running text. - **What should it feel like?** Joyful, hand-made, bold. - **What must it not look like?** A torn or rough edge (scissors cut clean), a tidy geometric sans with a filter on it, a script, or a tracing of any artwork. The work starts from the description of cut paper: flat unshaded color, long straight snips, curves turned in a few facets, shapes that lean. - **Which styles?** The person did not say, so I chose: one weight, the heaviest that keeps its counters open, in two families. `Cutout` has one color, so the person can color it. `Cutout Color` gives each letter its own sheet of paper and has three palettes.
