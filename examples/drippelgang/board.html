Drippelgang: 2 directions

Make me a font for a Halloween party. Dip every letter in slime and let it drip. Spooky and fun, no gore. - **What is it for?** One or two lines at a large size: a party invitation, a poster for a school event, a sign on the door. Not for running text. (I chose this.) - **What should it feel like?** Spooky, gooey, fun. It must say Halloween at a glance and still read as letters. (I chose this.) - **What must it not look like?** Blood or gore, a distressed horror font that is hard to read, or a plain font with a Halloween picture beside it. (I chose this.) - **Which styles?** One style in each family. Drippelgang is a color font with four palettes. Drippelgang Ink is the same letters in one color. A color font has no variable file. (I chose this.)
