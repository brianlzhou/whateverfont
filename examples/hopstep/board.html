Hopstep: 3 directions

A font of people in the manner of 1980s New York subway chalk drawings and pop street art: simple faceless figures drawn with one thick even line, bodies in flat bright colors, caught mid-dance, with short dashes around them that show movement. The idea is a human alphabet. Each capital letter is one figure, or two where a letter needs it, whose body makes the shape of that letter, so that people can type real words and the words dance. `DANCE`, `LOVE`, `PARTY` and `HELLO` must read as words at 100 px and still be clearly made of people. Lowercase letters are the same figures, so that typing in lowercase works.
