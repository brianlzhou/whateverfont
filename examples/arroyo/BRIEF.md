# Arroyo

## Brief

A serif for a long policy essay that is read on screen and printed as a PDF. It should feel like a flare serif
(stems that widen toward their ends, no brackets or slabs) and must not look like a default web serif. It needs
a light and a bold, an italic, and it has to stay readable at 16px. It pairs with a neutral sans for captions.

## Decisions

- Started from the `flare` preset: Roman capital proportions, open apertures, thin-to-thick ratio of 0.6.
- Added a `FLAR` axis that runs from a plain sans (0) to a sharp wedge (100). One file covers a quiet body
  text and a more incised headline.
- The italic is a slanted roman at 11 degrees, narrowed by 4.5%. A drawn cursive italic is future work.
- Three directions go on the review board: the calm default, a sharper `Glyphic`, and a sturdier `Text`.
