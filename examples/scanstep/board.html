Scanstep Color: 2 directions

Make me a pixel font like the text in an 8-bit video game. - **What it is for:** the text of a game, or of a page that looks like one: a title screen, "PRESS START", a score line, a menu, a dialog box. Also two or three lines of plain text at 16 or 24 pixels. (I chose this.) - **How it should feel:** chunky, crisp and exact, like text on an old television set. Every pixel is a square of the same size on one grid. - **What it must not look like:** a smooth font with a pixel texture on top, soft or half pixels, stems of uneven width, or letters that work one at a time but not in a line. - **Styles and palettes:** Scanstep, in one color, and Scanstep Color, with the same pixels, a hard shadow and a darker foot, in four palettes: Title, Dungeon, Handheld and Manual. One style in each family. A color font has no variable file.
