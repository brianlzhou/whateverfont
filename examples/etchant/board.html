Etchant: 2 directions

Make me a font like the traces on a circuit board. I read this as a color font in which each letter is drawn as copper traces on a printed circuit board. The strokes are thin traces of one even width. They run straight and turn in 45 degree bends. Each free end stops in a round solder pad with a drilled hole, and a via (a small ring with a hole) sits at some of the joints. The copper lies on a dark board under a lighter halo of solder mask. - **What is it for?** A word or a short line at a large size: a poster, a label, a sticker, a hardware project, a maker event, a slide. It still reads at 28 px. (I chose this.) - **What should it feel like?** Engineered, tidy, a little playful. It should look like a real board when you zoom in. (I chose this.) - **What must it not look like?** Round neon tubes, a pixel font, or a schematic drawing with symbols. The letters must not turn into a maze in which the word is lost. (I chose this.) - **Which styles?** One style in each family. `Etchant` is the color font, with four palettes: Green, Black, Blue and Paper. `Etchant Plain` is the same traces in one color. A color font has no variable file. (I chose this.)
