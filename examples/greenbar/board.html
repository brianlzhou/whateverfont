Greenbar: 2 directions

Monospaced fonts with more character than a plain coding face, in the spirit of the return to typewriter and early-computer looks that recent coding fonts made popular. This one reads the brief as what computers printed and showed. - **What it is for:** anything that should look printed by a computer around 1985: an invoice, a boarding pass, a fan-fold listing, a receipt. And the same characters on the terminal that sent the job: a phosphor screen. (I chose both readings. The person did not say.) - **How it should feel:** mechanical and exact. Every dot on one grid. - **What it must not look like:** a pixel font with square pixels, or dots scattered at random. - **Styles:** Greenbar Regular and Bold (the printer's emphasized mode), and Halation, a color font with Green, Amber and White palettes for dark backgrounds, and Paper for a light page.
