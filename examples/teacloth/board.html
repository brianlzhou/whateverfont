Teacloth: 2 directions

Make me a font like the cross-stitch letters on a sampler that hangs in a kitchen. - **What is it for?** Short lines at a large size: "Home Sweet Home", a name and a date, an alphabet row, a kitchen sign, a card, a label for a jar. It still reads at 28 px, but it is not for running text. (I chose this.) - **How should it feel?** Homemade, warm and patient: thread on linen, every letter worked square by square. (I chose this, from the request.) - **What must it not look like?** A pixel font with a stitch texture laid over it, a printed pattern, or letters too blurred by the stitches to read. (I chose this.) - **Which styles and palettes?** One style in each family. Two families: `Teacloth`, and `Teacloth Initials` with a framed capital for each letter A to Z. Four palettes, each made for its own cloth: Linen (red on natural linen, the showpiece), Blue (blue on white), Garden (bands of green, rose and gold) and Night (white on dark cloth). A color font has no variable file. (I chose this.)
