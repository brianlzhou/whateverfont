Crate Stencil: 3 directions

Make me a stencil font for labels on shipping crates — bold, a bit condensed, with the stencil bridges cut through the letters like real spray-paint stencils. I need a Regular and a Bold. - **What it is for:** short labels sprayed or printed on wooden shipping crates: words like FRAGILE, THIS SIDE UP, crate numbers, port names. Mostly capitals and figures, read from a few meters away. - **How it should feel:** sturdy, industrial, utilitarian. (Chosen: the person said "bold" and "real spray-paint stencils", so I read that as practical and heavy, not decorative.) - **What it must not look like:** a decorative "stencil effect" with thin hairline gaps, or a military costume font with rough edges. The bridges must be wide enough to be real (a cut sheet needs them) and every closed counter (A B D O P Q R 0 4 6 8 9, a b d e g o p q) must be opened by a bridge, because a real stencil cannot hold a floating counter. - **Styles:** Regular and Bold, as asked. (Chosen: no italic, because crate labels are never italic. The Regular is already heavy, because the person asked for a bold font; the Bold is heavier still.)
