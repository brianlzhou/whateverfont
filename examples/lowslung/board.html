Lowslung

Make me a wide, flat sans for the name of a studio. Low and stretched, like the lettering on the back of a sports car. - **What it is for:** the name of a studio, a label or a shop. One to three words at 60 to 160 pixels, in capitals or in mixed case, and a short paragraph at 18 pixels under them. - **How it should feel:** low and stretched. The letters sit wide on the line and their strokes end flat. - **What it must not look like:** a narrow headline face, or a futuristic face with broken letters. - **Styles:** Light, Regular, Medium, Bold and Extrabold, and one variable font with a weight axis (300 to 800) and a width axis (70 to 125). No italic: the request did not ask for one.
