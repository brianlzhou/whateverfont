# Caliper and Gantry

## Brief

Titles for the printed packets and one-page handouts of a robotics event. The pages are white with one dark
band, the titles are large and light, and the body text is a neutral sans. The title face should be a
condensed flare serif in light weights. A second face is wanted for numbers, labels and diagrams: technical,
squared, and calm.

## Decisions

- Caliper starts from the `flare` preset at 70% width and a 62-unit stem. The default style is Light because
  the titles are set large.
- The capitals sit halfway between even and Roman proportions (`classical=0.55`). Full Roman widths looked
  uneven once the letters were this narrow.
- Caliper has a width axis from 60 to 100 so one file serves a compressed headline and a normal subtitle.
- Gantry starts from the `technical` preset. Its `ROND` axis rounds every corner, so the same face can be
  strict on a spec sheet and friendly on a badge.
